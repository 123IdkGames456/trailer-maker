The video is uploaded to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Do not upload private or sensitive footage.

02 / Choose the vibe

Choose one or more of the 10 main vibes. Each opens 10 specific clip types. Or press AI Choose and Gemini will pick the best vibes, choose matching sub-options, and find clips automatically.

You can mix categories, for example Horror + Mystery, or History + Serious Drama. AI Choose will select from the footage, not from a random preset.

03 / Your matching clips

Choose which moments you want to keep.

Preview uses the original uploaded video. Download Selected Clips cuts the chosen moments into separate MP4 files in your browser.
